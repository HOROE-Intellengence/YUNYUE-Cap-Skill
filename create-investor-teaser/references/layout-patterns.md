# Investor Teaser Layout Patterns

Read this reference after the user ranks the 2–3 candidate highlights. Every pattern is fixed to `9:20 vertical, 1440 × 3200 px, 2K/QHD+ class`; never adapt these patterns to another aspect ratio or lower-resolution canvas.

Global visual constraint for every pattern:

- target 25–35% of the canvas for purely skeuomorphic imagery and never exceed 35%;
- keep at least 65% information-bearing typography, numbers, charts, matrices, timelines, flows, labeled diagrams, sources, and whitespace;
- a labeled technical cutaway or evidence chart is explanatory; decorative or unlabeled realism counts toward the 35% cap;
- Rank 1 always appears before and larger than Rank 2; Rank 2 always appears before and larger than Rank 3.

## High-impact poster rhythm

Use 5–8 coherent modules in this general rhythm:

1. location/company/round identity strip;
2. oversized category headline plus one cohesive hero scene;
3. financing badge or capital-scale band;
4. one three-number proof grid or compact growth chart;
5. Rank 2 evidence module;
6. Rank 3 evidence module;
7. moat, process loop, source note, and CTA as needed.

Use luminous white or ice-blue backgrounds, saturated cobalt/electric blue, deep navy, and restrained metallic gold. Allow controlled 3D headline treatment, blue gradient bands, lightly rounded glass panels, glossy icons, rim lighting, glow, arrows, and translucent chart columns. Keep evidence hierarchy stronger than decoration.

## Team-first banker matrix

Use when employer pedigree, product ownership, technical completeness, or mass-production history is the strongest risk reducer.

Recommended fixed 9:20 allocation when team is Rank 1:

- 5% identity and financing line;
- 34% Rank 1 headline, credential matrix, and supporting hero scene;
- 20% Rank 2 proof module;
- 14% Rank 3 proof module;
- 17% capital-scale band or explanatory product/platform diagram;
- 10% market/capital validation, sources, and footer.

Use `member/role | evidence-bearing history | transferable capability`. Avoid headshots, decorative people, and three independent biography cards. If silhouettes are requested, keep one group under 3% of the canvas.

## Traction-first proof stack

Use when company-level revenue, orders, customers, certifications, deployments, or retention is strong.

Recommended fixed 9:20 allocation when traction is Rank 1:

- 5% identity and financing line;
- 34% result headline, decisive number, and customer/deployment hero;
- 21% Rank 2 proof module;
- 14% Rank 3 proof module;
- 17% compact growth chart or capital-scale band;
- 9% source notes, transaction CTA, and footer.

Do not mix team historical delivery with current company traction. Preserve anonymous customer masks and NDA labels.

## Market-first conclusion number

Use when a structural industry shift is the strongest hook and the market data has a defensible source and definition.

Recommended fixed 9:20 allocation when market is Rank 1:

- 5% identity and financing line;
- 34% market verdict, decisive number, and category-scale hero;
- 21% Rank 2 proof module;
- 14% Rank 3 proof module;
- 17% market-trigger or growth visualization;
- 9% proof qualification, source note, and footer.

Show year, geography, definition, historical versus forecast status, and a source marker. Do not present TAM alone; include one short line explaining why the value pool is forming.

## Product-first dual object

Use when two products, modules, or capabilities combine into a clear platform thesis.

Recommended fixed 9:20 allocation when product is Rank 1:

- 5% identity and financing line;
- 32% product verdict, oversized headline, and cohesive hero composition;
- 20% labeled mechanism diagram or three-number proof grid;
- 18% Rank 2 proof module;
- 12% Rank 3 proof module;
- 9% capital-scale band;
- 4% sources, transaction CTA, and footer.

Treat two products as one explanatory system, connected only by a real relationship such as sensing × execution, hardware × software, or device × recurring service. Do not create two competing product advertisements. Purely realistic product objects still count toward the 35% canvas cap.

## Capital-validation-first

Use when a strategic lead investor, verified acquisition precedent, or adjacent-company rerating validates the asset category.

Recommended fixed 9:20 allocation when capital validation is Rank 1:

- 5% identity and financing line;
- 34% capital event or benchmark verdict plus category-scale hero;
- 22% milestone-to-capital causal path;
- 17% Rank 2 proof module;
- 12% Rank 3 proof module;
- 10% sources, transaction CTA, and footer.

Use the precedent to prove the terminal value shape, not the project's current valuation. Never infer transaction terms or imply the project has already reached the benchmark's scale.

## Fixed vertical output

Do not provide a horizontal adaptation. Every image-generation prompt produced by this skill must remain `9:20 vertical, 1440 × 3200 px, 2K/QHD+ class` and explicitly request native high-resolution rendering with crisp Chinese typography.
